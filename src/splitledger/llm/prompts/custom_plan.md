Turn the user's description of a budget into plan parts.
Each part has a short name and either pct, a whole percent of monthly take home pay, or cap_cents, a money limit in cents with cap_per week or month.
categories lists what is spent from that part, chosen from Dining, Groceries, Transport, Shopping, Bills, Entertainment, Health, Travel, Other.
A savings part has a pct and no categories, because nothing is spent from it.
Food means Dining and Groceries unless they say otherwise.
If they start from a known plan such as 50/30/20 or the 60% solution, write out all of its parts with their changes. 50/30/20 is Needs 50 for Groceries, Transport, Bills, Health; Wants 30 for Dining, Entertainment, Shopping, Travel, Other; Savings 20.
Do not invent parts or limits they did not ask for. Do not add up or check the numbers.
period is weekly or monthly only when they say how often to check, otherwise null.
